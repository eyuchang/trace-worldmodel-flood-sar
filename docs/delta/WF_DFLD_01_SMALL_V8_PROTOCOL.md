## Purpose and scope

V8 remediates four material development-audit findings: repeated incidents from a
single persistent eligibility state, overly permissive online call merging,
completion-time truncation at the six-hour boundary, and incomplete bindings at
the predictor and registered-validation boundaries. The scientific sequence is:

1. form auditable eligibility episodes and thin keyed incident candidates;
2. refit truth and observation coefficients using only the registered development
   seeds;
3. select one conservative visible-evidence graph from three preregistered spatial
   candidates using fixed five-fold rules;
4. freeze all mechanics, coefficients, source hashes, evaluation endpoints, gates,
   and the canonical environment;
5. derive and publish `confirmatory-v7` without executing it locally;
6. after an authorized push, execute the original remote workflow exactly once and
   publish every result without retuning.

The August deliverable remains Tasks 1 and 2: a common Toy/MLP/V-JEPA predictor
boundary and a complete runnable Small simulator. V8 does not train a Delta JEPA,
add breach or cascade physics, model negotiated mutual aid, add duty cycles,
federate runtimes, add a casualty model, or add a new UI.

## Incident-episode definition

Accepted structure/tick/type candidates are sorted by onset, type, structure,
infrastructure, and candidate digest. A continuous eligibility episode is keyed by
the incident type and affected structure or infrastructure plus the current
eligible subject set. One incident may be accepted within an episode. Eligibility
must clear, or its subject set must change, before a new episode may begin.
Different incident types remain distinct.

The hidden candidate audit records every evaluated candidate's digest, probability,
draw digest, episode key, disposition, and suppression reason. It is unavailable to
the controller and excluded from public TRACE evidence.

## Observation and reconciliation protocol

The observation channel remains keyed, zero/one/many, lossy, and derived only after
truth. Nonunique taxonomy-conditioned descriptor families provide weak visible
evidence; they never encode a truth identifier.

The controller represents proposed relationships as `confirmed`, `suspected`,
`rejected`, or `superseded` links. Valid explicit revisions and exact available
callback tokens are hard confirmations. A soft confirmation requires temporal,
spatial, and taxonomy compatibility, one additional descriptor or occupant/medical
agreement, exactly one eligible destination cluster, a resulting span no greater
than 1,800 seconds, and no visible contradiction.

Before selection, the spatial multipliers are frozen as `q=0.75`, `q=1.00`, and
`q=1.25`. Development seeds are partitioned by seed index modulo five. A candidate
is eligible only if each fold lowers false-merge rate relative to the immutable v7
baseline while losing no more than 0.05 absolute recall. Eligible candidates are
ordered by aggregate false-merge rate, then F1, then smaller `q`. The documented
fallback and failure rules in the acceptance protocol are binding.

The untouched holdout comparison's primary endpoint is the seed-level paired
difference in false-merge rate. Pairwise recall has a -0.05 noninferiority margin.
Only a false-merge upper 95% interval below zero and a recall-difference lower 95%
interval above -0.05 support an improvement claim.

## Outcomes and capacity

Service completion is never truncated to the scenario horizon. Each authorized
service records scheduled completion, observed completion when inside the window,
the six-hour censoring time, and either `completed_within_window` or
`active_at_scenario_censoring`. Resources remain busy through their untruncated
scheduled completion.

Strict one-resource/one-incident concurrency remains the primary load definition.
Uncapped compatible service-unit load and the historical normalized coverable-load
index remain labeled sensitivity measures. No resource is added or reclassified to
produce a preferred value.

## Predictor and provenance rules

When the revalidation guard is enabled, evidence must bind the exact predictor
version, model hash, calibration version, calibration hash, and action class.
Missing, mismatched, unregistered, pending, superseded, or cross-schema evidence
fails closed. The Toy predictor remains a transparent teaching fixture. MLP and
V-JEPA remain unqualified without an exact, verified qualification artifact.

Predictor requests bind the full visible observation, nearest registered gauge,
stage and sample time, crossing state and sample age, call/coordination age,
weather, registered compatible resource telemetry, prior profile, and any visual
feature reference. Automatic aid is always labeled preauthorized automatic aid.

## Validation discipline and claims

Only the 100 inherited development seeds may be used for coefficient fitting,
algorithm selection, and debugging. The confirmatory-v7 seeds do not exist until
all mechanics, selected reconciliation algorithm, coefficients, code and data
hashes, gates, and canonical environment are frozen and committed.

The original confirmatory run is remote, write-once, and bound to its exact source
commit, complete scientific-input manifest, digest-pinned Python 3.11.14 image,
hash-locked dependencies, and workflow run ID. Later executions are replications.
Failed gates or adverse endpoints are published without seed replacement or
retuning.

Supported claims concern deterministic simulator mechanics, provenance,
truth/observation separation, controlled axis behavior, TRACE governance, and
synthetic-process evaluation. V8 is not a hydrodynamic forecast, demographic or
historical-event calibration, operational availability claim, learned-model
effectiveness study, or emergency-response readiness assessment.
